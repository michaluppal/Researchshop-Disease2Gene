\subsection{Applications}

\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Automated literature overview:} Converts a documented query or selected paper set into a source-linked overview of relevant papers, genes, variants, and extracted findings before focused manual appraisal.
    \item \textbf{Variant of Uncertain Significance (VUS) evidence triage:} VUSs are common in large hereditary-disease testing cohorts, and reclassification can take months or years~\cite{chen2023vus}. ResearchShop can collect source-grounded literature findings around a specified gene or variant for expert assessment, but it does not classify or reclassify variants.
    \item \textbf{Rare disease evidence triage:} Helps process curated PMID sets or focused PubMed searches where relevant reports are sparse and require source-level review.
    \item \textbf{Drug target discovery:} Helps researchers assemble candidate gene--disease association tables for therapeutic-development review.
    \item \textbf{Systematic reviews:} Produces auditable extraction tables that can support, but not replace, documented review and adjudication workflows.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Free-tier Execution Study}

We ran the same 10 open-access PMIDs hourly for 24 Warsaw-local slots on a Windows laptop using WSL, one worker, and a three-call cap per paper. The tested free-tier model was \texttt{gemini-3.1-flash-lite}; the released application defaults to \texttt{gemini-2.5-flash-lite}. The study measures operational reliability and output stability, not biological precision or recall. The condition was operationally usable: 23/24 batches completed, with no quota-limited rows or timeouts. Across successful runs, candidate validation retained 7,259 of 8,570 candidate records; final row decisions emitted 7,007 rows and excluded 353 at the citation gate, with no strict-gate drops. These independent operational counts do not estimate precision or recall. The logs recorded 95 recovered model-unavailable/API events; these are recovery log events, not necessarily 95 unique failed requests, so retries and explicit failure reporting remain required.

\subsection{Limitations}

LLM non-determinism limits exact reproducibility, and the workflow depends on PubMed discovery, open-access full text, and an external Gemini service. Citation- and journal-based ranking may also deprioritise low-citation rare-disease reports; direct PMID lists or documented focused queries are preferable in that setting.

Future work includes an expert-labelled benchmark, alternative search sources, and local-model support for fully private processing. All outputs remain research leads requiring professional review.
